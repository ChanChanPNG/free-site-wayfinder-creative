05 / HEALTHY SKEPTICISM WELCOME

Still skeptical?

Here is every question you have, answered. We really do just wanna give away a free site.

Is this actually free?

Yes. For the selected business, design, development, launch, and hosting are covered. Domain purchase and renewals are not included.

What's the catch?

There's no required purchase. We're sponsoring a local business to support our community and showcase our work. You tell us your story; we choose a business we believe we can help.

Is hosting really free forever?

Yes. Hosting for the sponsored website is free forever. No recurring hosting bill from Wayfinder Creative. Your domain purchase and renewals remain your responsibility.

What about my domain?

Already have one? We'll help connect it. If you need one, we'll help you get it. Domain registration and renewal fees are paid by you and are separate from the free website and hosting.

How do you choose the business?

We review each business's story, website needs, and the potential impact a new website could have. This is a sponsorship selection, not a random drawing or popularity contest.

Who can apply?

Businesses in our local community that could benefit from a new website. Already have an outdated site? Need your first one? Either way, tell us why a new site would make a difference.

Can I apply if I already have a website?

Absolutely. Replacing an outdated website is one of the goals you can choose. Tell us what's not working and what you'd like your new site to do.

Does everyone who applies get a website?

No. One local business will be selected for this sponsorship. Applying doesn't guarantee selection or a free website.
